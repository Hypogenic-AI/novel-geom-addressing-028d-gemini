# Downloaded Papers

This directory contains research papers relevant to the study of novel geometry addressing in LLMs.

1. **Beyond Lines and Circles: Unveiling the Geometric Reasoning Gap in Large Language Models** (2024)
   - Authors: Spyridon Mouselinos, H. Michalewski, Mateusz Malinowski
   - Relevance: Investigates LLMs' abilities in 2D spatial relationships and geometric reasoning.
   
2. **TangramPuzzle: Evaluating Multimodal Large Language Models with Compositional Spatial Reasoning** (2026)
   - Authors: Daixian Liu et al.
   - Relevance: Uses symbolic geometric framework (TCE) for coordinate specifications.

3. **Minimization of Boolean Complexity in In-Context Concept Learning** (2024)
   - Authors: Leroy Z. Wang, R. Thomas McCoy, Shane Steinert-Threlkeld
   - Relevance: Explores simplicity bias in ICL, which might relate to how "repeated" geometries are addressed.

4. **In-Context Learning Operates as Concept Subspace Learning** (2026)
   - Authors: Wei Tang et al.
   - Relevance: Provides a mechanistic view of how tasks (potentially including geometry) are represented in-context.

5. **The Geometry of Reasoning: Flowing Logics in Representation Space** (2025)
   - Authors: Yufa Zhou et al.
   - Relevance: Models reasoning as flows in representation space, connecting logic with geometric quantities.
